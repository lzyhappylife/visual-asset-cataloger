# Visual annotation rubric

Use the user’s exact field names when provided. The following rubric is a default for couple or portrait asset catalogs and can be adapted to another domain without changing the evidence rules.

| Field | Record only visible evidence | Good evidence examples |
|---|---|---|
| 婚纱照整体风格 | Overall visual treatment, palette, wardrobe pairing, set or backdrop | red-gold traditional motifs, dark studio background, high-key gray backdrop |
| 人物数量 | Count of visible people; mention partial or obscured figures | `1人：画面仅见一名女性` |
| 新娘服饰 / 新郎服饰 | Garment color, cut, sleeve or collar, pattern, accessories, shoes when visible | white puff sleeves, black double-breasted jacket, veil, embroidered red fabric |
| 新娘的动作 / 新郎的动作 | Body orientation, pose, hand placement, supported object, crop limits | seated on a bench, holding bouquet at chest, body turned toward the other person |
| 互动动作 | Visible contact or coordinated action between people | holding hands, forehead touching, carrying, standing apart with no visible contact |
| 视线互动情况 | Eye direction and whether mutual gaze is visible | both look at camera, one person faces away, eyes hidden by profile or veil |
| 情绪释放强度 | Visible facial expression and pose energy; do not infer inner feelings | open-mouth smile, closed lips, neutral face, raised arms and dynamic posture |
| 接触元素 | Objects or surfaces touched by people and clearly visible body contact | bench, bouquet, veil, shared hand grip, contact hidden by crop |
| 背景元素 | Visible setting, furniture, props, lights, wall or floor | studio lamps, mural, gray wall, white pedestal, confetti on floor |
| 构图 | Orientation, subject placement, crop, symmetry, negative space, leading lines | vertical full-body, subject on right with left-side negative space, bench as horizontal line |
| 镜头感 | Apparent distance and viewpoint only when visually supported; state when focal length cannot be known | close portrait, eye-level full-body view, rear-view foreground figure, focal length无法判断 |
| 光线 | Visible color, direction, contrast, highlights, shadow, flare | warm side light, high-key diffuse light, dark background with rim light |
| 水印状态 | Presence, visible text, position, and overlap | `有水印：下方中央白色文字覆盖裙摆` or `未见水印` |
| 整体置信度 | One numeric evidence-quality score from 0 to 1 in the rightmost column | `0.96` |

When content is not confirmable, use a precise uncertainty statement such as `无法判断：男方背对镜头，眼睛不可见` or `无法确认：手部被裙摆遮挡`. Do not replace uncertainty with a likely interpretation.

Avoid these unsupported shortcuts:

- `正在互动` without naming the visible contact or coordinated action
- `姿势自然`, `场景普通`, `氛围很好`, or other non-observable judgments
- claims about marital status, identity, intention, personality, or the unseen continuation of an action
- a field value copied from another image because the set looks similar
